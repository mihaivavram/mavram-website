Inspired by <a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rotten Tomatoes</a> and purposed for gaming - The GamePass Picker is a service to help gamers find their next favorite game to play on the Microsoft GamePass service based on their past gaming behaviors and preferences. Eventually, my goal is to turn this project into a platform to help gamers manage their play time, preferences, achievements, and impact with gaming.
