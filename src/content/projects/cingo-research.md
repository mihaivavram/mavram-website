Cingo Research is an app that uses mobile sensor data—such as activity levels, location patterns, and device usage—to better understand the daily experiences of young adults. By combining this real-world context with quick, science-based mood check-ins, the app can help identify patterns, predict emotional shifts, and suggest strategies for improving wellbeing.
