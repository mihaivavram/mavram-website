Elebase is a streamlined content and geospatial data management platform designed for creating interactive mapping applications quickly and affordably. It enables users to build no‑code interactive maps, publish GeoJSON and tilesets, and integrate geodata enriched with media—all via an intuitive interface or its API. With Elebase, you can easily define your data, invite non‑technical contributors, and visualize it seamlessly through template apps, geo files, or the API.
